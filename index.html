Who We Are

A School Built Around Every Child

BBS International School is a co-educational institution located near Dahiyawan Mela Ground, Prayagraj. We offer quality education from Kindergarten to Class 8, with a curriculum designed to build strong academic foundations alongside confidence, creativity and good character.

Our campus is designed to be a safe, welcoming space where children feel encouraged to ask questions, explore ideas and grow at their own pace. Small class sizes and caring teachers ensure every student receives the attention they deserve.

FAQ

Common Academic Questions

What is the medium of instruction?

English is the primary medium of instruction, with Hindi taught as a subject from primary classes onwards.

What classes are offered?

BBS International School offers classes from Kindergarten (KG) to Class 8.

How are students assessed?

Assessment is continuous and includes classwork, projects, periodic tests and term examinations.

Is there homework support?

Yes — teachers provide structured homework and are available to guide students when needed.

Admission Process

Simple 4-Step Process

Step 1 — Enquiry

Call us at 9236008658 or visit the school campus to enquire about admissions for your child's class.

Step 2 — Campus Visit

Schedule a visit to see our classrooms, facilities and meet our teachers. We'll be happy to answer all your questions.

Step 3 — Application Form

Collect and fill out the admission form. Submit it along with the required documents (birth certificate, previous school records, photographs).

Step 4 — Confirmation

After review, you'll receive admission confirmation. Complete the fee formalities and your child is ready to join!
